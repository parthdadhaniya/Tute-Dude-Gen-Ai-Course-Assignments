ACME CORP - EMPLOYEE POLICY & OPERATIONAL HANDBOOK

Section 1: Working Hours & Remote Work
Employees work standard core hours from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Acme Corp operates on a flexible hybrid work model. Full-time team members are eligible
to work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Remote work requires a
stable internet connection and adherence to company data privacy standards. All team
members must be available on communication channels during core collaboration hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM).

Section 2: Annual Leave & Time Off Policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave is accrued on a monthly pro-rata basis (1.66 days per month worked).
Employees can carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year,
which must be utilized before March 31st of that following year. Any remaining unused leave beyond
the 5-day limit will lapse without cash compensation. Leave requests longer than 3 consecutive
business days must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

Section 3: Sick Leave & Medical Benefits
All employees are entitled to 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year.
Sick leave does not carry forward into the subsequent calendar year and cannot be encashed.
If sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive working days, an official medical certificate signed by
a registered healthcare practitioner must be uploaded to the HR portal upon returning to work.
Acme Corp provides comprehensive health insurance coverage for all full-time employees, their
spouses, and dependent children up to 21 years old.

Section 4: Professional Development & Learning Allowance
Acme Corp encourages continuous professional growth. Every employee is allocated an annual
learning budget of $1,500 for certifications, technical courses, industry conferences, and books.
To utilize this allowance, the employee must submit a course outline and business justification
to their department head. Pre-approval is required prior to purchasing materials. Approved expenses
are reimbursed in the subsequent monthly payroll cycle upon submitting valid tax receipts.

Section 5: Hardware & Device Security Policy
All company-issued laptops (MacBook Pro or Dell XPS) are protected by endpoint encryption and
remote wipe capability. Employees must never install unapproved third-party software or disable
antivirus agents. Personal devices accessing company email or repositories must comply with two-factor
authentication (2FA) and multi-factor authentication (MFA) requirements. Lost or stolen hardware must
be reported to IT Security within 2 hours of discovery.
